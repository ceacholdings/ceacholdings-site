Call notifications by text

Business owners who subscribe to the CEAC Holdings AI receptionist can receive a text message after each call we answer for them. Each text summarizes the call: the time, its length, the caller's name and number where available, what the caller wanted, and whether a live transfer reached you. If a caller asks for an appointment we cannot add to your calendar, you also get a text asking you to book it by hand.

Text notifications are optional. You can use our service without them, and consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchase.

How to sign up

Fill in the form below with your business name, your name and your mobile number. To receive texts, tick the consent box. The box is not ticked for you and is not required to submit the form.

The consent statement on the form reads:

I agree to receive call notification texts from CEAC Holdings at the number provided. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy and SMS Terms.

Form not showing? Open the sign-up form on Google Forms.

After you sign up

You will receive one text confirming your subscription. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Questions: support@ceacholdings.com.
