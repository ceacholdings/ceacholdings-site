An AI receptionist that answers every call

CEAC Holdings provides an AI phone receptionist for small service businesses such as plumbers, electricians and dental offices. When the business cannot pick up — after hours, on a job, or on another line — our receptionist answers, takes the caller's details, books appointments where the business allows it, and transfers urgent calls to the owner.

Call notifications by text

Business owners who use our service can choose to receive a text message after each call our receptionist answers for them, summarizing who called and why. Owners sign up on our call notifications page. See our SMS Terms for details.
