An AI receptionist that answers every call

CEAC Holdings provides an AI phone receptionist for small service businesses such as plumbers, electricians and dental offices. When you cannot pick up, our receptionist answers for you.

Call (202) 644-7965 support@ceacholdings.com

How it works

  1. You can't pick up

    It's after hours, you're on a job, or you're on another line.

  2. Our receptionist answers

    It takes the caller's details, books appointments where you allow it, and transfers urgent calls to you.

  3. You get a summary

    If you opt in, a text after each call tells you who called and why.

Call notifications by text

Business owners who use our service can choose to receive a text message after each call our receptionist answers for them, summarizing who called and why. Owners sign up on our call notifications page. See our SMS Terms for details.
