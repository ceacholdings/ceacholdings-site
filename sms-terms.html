Program name

CEAC Holdings Call Notifications.

What we send

CEAC Holdings answers phone calls on behalf of the small businesses that subscribe to our AI receptionist service. Business owners who opt in receive a text message after each call we answer for them, summarizing the call: the time, its length, the caller's name and phone number where available, a short summary of what the caller wanted, and whether a live transfer reached the owner. If a caller asks for an appointment that our receptionist cannot add to the owner's calendar, the owner also receives a text asking them to book it by hand, with the requested time, the service, the ZIP code where given, and why the booking was not made. These are transactional account notifications. We do not send marketing or promotional messages through this program.
